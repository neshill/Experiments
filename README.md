# Fali Ti Jedan Papir — playable prototype

A bureaucratic roguelike deck-builder about paperwork, patience and "you're missing one paper".
Web prototype (v17): phone portrait plus a landscape layout for desktop and handheld screens (designed at 1280×800). Open `index.html` in any browser; on a phone, Share → Add to Home Screen plays it full-screen.

**v17:** new title screen (Start game / Rules / Quit), you choose which cards to keep at the end of every turn (keep none to redraw all 5), and gossip now pops up as speech bubbles from the clerk.

**How to play (touch):** tap a card to play it, hold to preview, slide across the hand to browse, slide up out of the hand to play the previewed card. Tap the round button to end your turn; the red segments around it are the turns left before the window closes.

**Mouse:** hover to preview, click to play.
**Keyboard:** 1–9 play a card, Space/E end turn, Esc cancel/close, M map, C cards.
