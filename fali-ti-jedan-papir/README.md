# Fali Ti Jedan Papir — playable prototype

A bureaucratic roguelike deck-builder about paperwork, patience and "you're missing one paper".
Phone-first web prototype (v15.1). Open `index.html` in any browser; on a phone, Share → Add to Home Screen plays it full-screen.

**How to play:** tap a card to play it, hold to preview, slide across the hand to browse, slide up out of the hand to play the previewed card. Tap the round button to end your turn; the red segments around it are the turns left before the window closes.
